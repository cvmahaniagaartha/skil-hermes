# Urocare Style Memory

Status: provisional editorial memory; brand-scoped to Urocare only.

## User's preferred output style

- Use concise, natural, conversational Indonesian.
- Sound like a clinician explaining directly to a person, not like a shortened medical article.
- Give the answer early.
- Explain the medical mechanism in plain language.
- Add concrete risk factors or everyday situations.
- Use a short quoted audience question or dialogue when it makes the post feel real.
- Reassure without dismissing the concern.
- End with practical steps and when to seek medical evaluation.
- Keep the main draft clean; put medical-review notes outside the copy.
- Use jokes only when they emerge naturally and do not trivialize the condition.

## Strong Urocare mechanisms

1. Partner perspective: address wives or couples trying to understand a husband's sexual or urological symptoms.
2. Suspicion reversal: a wife suspects infidelity, then the post introduces shame, fear of failure, or a possible health issue as an alternative explanation.
3. Private worry -> remove shame -> practical next step.
4. Direct question -> simple medical explanation -> reassurance -> evaluation threshold.
5. Behavioral contradiction: the audience is willing to buy supplements or search online but avoids a proper consultation.
6. Audience-comment continuation: start from a real question such as “Dok, cowok itu bisa pura-pura ngaceng ya?” and answer it with one natural joke plus responsible education.

## Preferred explanatory sequence

Direct answer -> plain-language explanation -> concrete context -> quoted audience question -> reassurance -> practical action -> when to consult.

## Examples supplied by the user

### Example: age-related erection education

“Memasuki usia 45 tahun ke atas, pria memang bisa mengalami perubahan kualitas ereksi. Butuh waktu lebih lama untuk mengeras, kekerasannya tidak seperti dulu, atau lebih sulit mempertahankan ereksi.

Itu disebabkan karena seiring bertambahnya usia, kesehatan pembuluh darah, fungsi saraf, dan kadar hormon testosteron bisa mengalami perubahan. Belum lagi kalau punya riwayat diabetes, kolesterol tinggi, hipertensi, atau kebiasaan merokok sejak muda.

🗣️: “Jadi kalau umur udah 45+ kita sebagai pria cuma bisa pasrah dok?”

Bukan seperti itu, pria usia 45, 50, bahkan 60 tahun ke atas masih bisa memiliki ereksi yang normal.

Kuncinya coba rutin olahraga, tidur cukup, berhenti merokok, dan jaga pola makan. Kalau keluhannya terus berulang, sebaiknya diperiksa karena gangguan ereksi juga bisa menjadi tanda awal masalah kesehatan pembuluh darah dan jantung.”

Observed preference: flowing medical education with direct answer, plain explanation, concrete risk factors, audience dialogue, reassurance, practical steps, and evaluation threshold.

Sebagai dokter urologi, saya mau kasih tahu satu hal yang mungkin belum banyak istri sadari.

Sebagian pria yang mengalami disfungsi ereksi atau ejakulasi dini justru memilih menghindari hubungan intim karena takut mengecewakan pasangannya. Ada rasa malu, minder, sampai takut gagal lagi setiap kali diajak berhubungan.

Belum lagi kalau suami punya riwayat diabetes, tekanan darah tinggi, stres pekerjaan, atau kebiasaan merokok. Semua itu bisa memengaruhi kesehatan seksual pria.

Observed preference: partner-targeted hook, suspicion reversal, emotional tension, nonjudgmental framing, and joint action.

### Example: short introduction

Preferred concise introduction should mention the specific problems covered, for example: difficult erection, premature ejaculation, frequent urination, fertility, and other men's-health concerns. Avoid generic “melawan hoax” language when a more specific promise is available.

## Explicit preferences vs hypotheses

Explicit preferences:

- Conversational and concise Indonesian.
- Flowing explanatory structure.
- Specific risk factors and audience dialogue.
- Partner/wife audience can be relevant.
- Natural jokes are acceptable.
- Keep review notes outside the draft.

Low-confidence hypotheses:

- Partner-targeted suspicion-reversal hooks may attract more engagement when respectful.
- Short quoted audience questions may increase perceived authenticity.
- A natural joke can improve attention before a medical explanation, but humor must not weaken the seriousness of symptoms.

## Guardrails

- Do not make Urocare preferences global.
- Do not treat “lumayan memancing engage” as measured performance without metrics.
- Do not turn one correction into a permanent medical or editorial rule without approval.
- Keep all medical claims review-required until professionally verified.
- Editorial approval is not medical approval or publication authorization.
